The Three Hour Figures

Login: How long the person was clocked in, from Attendance.

Logged: How much of that they booked to a task.

Unlogged: The gap between login and logged hours. The key metric for audit.

Marks on a Day

unstopped timer? One entry runs over 12 hours — almost certainly a forgotten timer.

no attendance Time booked on a day with no clock-in at all.

over-booked More hours booked than the person was clocked in for.

still clocked in The day is still open. Login hours stay empty until clock-out.

auto-closed Closed by the 23:00 automatic sweep cutoff.

edited since The logs changed after the day was submitted for approval.

! In the team views, this day carries one of the marks above.

Approval (§22)

Not submitted Nothing sent for review yet.

Pending Waiting on a project manager or admin.

Approved Counted as reviewed time.

Rejected Sent back with a reason; employee re-submits.

Entries

Timer Recorded by starting and stopping the timer.

Manual Typed in by hand, or migrated from Workway.

Inactive The user account is suspended or left the company.
