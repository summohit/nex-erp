Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

Transparency First

This Privacy Policy outlines how NEX ERP collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use our enterprise management software and mobile application.

01

Introduction

Welcome to NEX ERP ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the privacy practices of our enterprise resource planning web and mobile applications (the "App").

02

Information We Collect

As an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform, we collect information required to facilitate organizational management and employment workflows:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, employee ID, and job details provided by you or your employer.
  • Location Data: When enabled, we collect location coordinates solely to support employee check-in, verified attendance tracking, and field visit logs.
  • Camera & Photo Access: Accessed only when you choose to upload profile pictures, scan badges, or attach documents (such as expense receipts or medical certificates).
  • Device & Usage Information: Device model, OS version, app crashes, and performance metrics to ensure enterprise reliability and troubleshooting.
03

How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate business and contractual purposes:

  • To authenticate users and safeguard accounts against unauthorized access.
  • To operate core business modules (attendance, HR, payroll, leaves, tasks, and project tracking).
  • To deliver critical system notifications, approvals, and employer communications.
  • To maintain audit trails, enhance platform security, and resolve technical issues.
04

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Data is shared exclusively under the following terms:

  • Your Employer: As an enterprise tool, data captured during work-related operations is accessible to your company's authorized administrators.
  • Infrastructure Partners: Secure cloud hosting, database, and notification service providers under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by lawful court orders, regulations, or regulatory authorities.
05

Data Security & Encryption

We implement industry-grade administrative, technical, and physical security standards. All communications between your device and our servers are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Sensitive storage is protected with role-based access control and token-based authentication.

06

Data Retention & User Rights

Your account data is retained for the duration of your organization's active service agreement. If you wish to request data deletion or account removal, please reach out to your company's system administrator or contact our support team.
