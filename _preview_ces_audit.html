Data quality findings

issues in the underlying sheet, not in project execution
01

Status contradicts logged progress

12 rows are flagged "Not Started" while carrying real completion percentages — three of them ("Pan 2.0 DR Mumbai", "F5 uDNS_Phase-12 Exp_ Hosar Road-", "PNB DC-Dwarka") are logged at 100% complete but still show as not started.

02

Department field is effectively unused

26 rows are blank ("--") and 46 carry an identical ~1,250-character "select every department" blob (3 near-duplicate copies). Only 9 rows carry a real, specific tag.

03

Category split by a typo

"Implementation and Deployment" (56) and a misspelled "Implentation and  Migration" (11) read as a typo fork of one taxonomy; 3 more rows carry no category at all.

04

Missing status on finished-looking work

4 rows have no Project Status set at all — all four are logged at 100% completion, so they're likely done and simply never marked.

05

Project Code inconsistent

26 of 81 rows (19 blank, 7 placeholder text like "On Premises-1") don't follow the CES/MMYY/### numbering the other 55 use.

06

Concentration risk on one lead

Gaurav Nogia appears on 40 of 81 projects (49%) — nearly every F5 uDNS rollout and half the DC/DR migrations name him, with no clear backup lead visible in the sheet.
